RafasConquest

A PvE addon for Villager Recruits: AI-run nations that hold land, raise armies from their territory, and go to war with each other and with you.

Download latest Source on GitHub

Pre-alpha · Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge 47.4+ · Java 17

What it adds

Living nations

Nations make decisions every day: they muster armies, march on their neighbours and declare wars, whether or not you're watching.

Battles

Armies of up to 100,000 soldiers fight in formation. Large armies split into a centre and two wings, each with its own orders.

Land titles

The world is divided into counties, duchies, kingdoms and empires, each with a seat city built into the terrain.

Characters and faiths

Every hold has a lord with stats, a culture and a faith. Sword Saints, Kings and Gods train at the sword sanctums.

World map

A map of the realm showing borders, roads, armies on the march and their war camps.

Roads and cities

Cobble, gravel and dirt roads with bridges and signposts link walled cities, towns, castles and hamlets.

Peerage

Climb the military and noble ladders: enlist with a nation, take quests from the boards, and earn a grant of land.

Bigger world

An optional horizontal stretch makes continents, mountains and biomes up to four times wider.

Install

  1. Install Forge 1.20.1 (47.4 or newer).
  2. Put Villager Recruits 1.15.2 in your mods folder. It's required.
  3. Download the latest RafasConquest jar and put it in mods too.
  4. Optional: Epic Knights gives each culture its own armour, and Distant Horizons and Embeddium let you see cities from far away.

When you make a new world, the RafasConquest button at the top right of the world creation screen holds the world's options.

Status

RafasConquest is in pre-alpha. Things change daily and saves may not carry over between versions. Every build is listed on the releases page.
